Travel

Since we’re dragging you out to Colorado, we’d love for everyone to turn this trip into a personal mini-vacation—with our wedding right in the middle.

Our wedding day is August 4th. The minimum travel window is August 3rd–5th, but we’d love for you to stay longer if you’re able—ideally August 2nd–6th.

We plan on exploring Colorado, organizing a few group outings, and spending quality time together outside of the wedding. You’re more than welcome to do your own thing, tag along with our plans, or mix and match. The 4th is our day. Everything else is yours.

We’ll be staying at the Trailborn Hotel from August 2nd–6th, which is about 5 minutes from the wedding venue.

  • Room blocks: August 3rd–5th
  • $229/night + tax (with discount)
  • Call Hotel (970-586-4471) to book the wedding block and recieve the discount

If you’d like to extend your stay (2nd–6th, 2nd–5th, 3rd–6th, or another arrangement), you can still receive the discounted rate—just make the request during your call with the hotel representative.

Below are a few alternative hotels in the area, and you’re also more than welcome to book an Airbnb or other accommodations if you prefer.

We can’t wait to celebrate and adventure with you!

Trailhead Lodge in Estes Park (Recommended stay)

The Estes Park Resort (Luxury alternative)

Holiday Inn (Cost Friendly Alternative)

All three options are within 5-10 minutes of the wedding venue and approximately 1.5 hours from Denver international Airport (The closest major airport)
